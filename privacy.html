Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Okulo is an app that answers questions about colours in a photo. This page explains what happens to your data. Short version: no account, no ads, no tracking, and we do not keep your photos.

Who we are

Okulo is made by Baptiste Corman and Nirvaan Thakur. Contact: baptiste.corman08@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Your colour test result (type and severity), your answer language and your settings.
  • Naming a colour by tapping the photo: this is done on your phone and nothing is sent.
  • Live camera modes (when available in your version): the camera picture is analysed on your phone, frame by frame, and nothing is sent or saved unless you freeze a frame and ask a question about it.

What is sent when you ask a question

Only after you allow it in the app, and only when you ask a question, Okulo sends:

  • the photo you are asking about (resized), and your question;
  • your colour test result and your answer language, so the answer fits you.

Your colour test result describes your eyesight, so it is only sent with your explicit permission, together with the question it helps to answer.

This goes to Okulo's server (hosted by Supabase), which forwards it to Claude, an AI service made by Anthropic, to write the answer, then sends the answer back to your phone. Okulo's server does not store your photos or your questions. Our hosting provider keeps short technical logs (such as the time of a request and error codes) to keep the service running.

Anthropic processes the photo and question to produce the answer, under its own terms and privacy policy: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

You can withdraw your permission at any time in the app (Profile, "Send photos to Claude for answers"). Okulo then only names colours on your phone.

Asking by voice

If you tap the microphone, Okulo uses Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone to turn what you say into text. Depending on your iPhone and language, Apple may process the audio on its servers, under Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy. Okulo does not record, keep or send your voice: only the text of your question is used, as described above. Okulo listens only while the microphone button is on.

What we do not do

  • No account, no name, no email address collected in the app.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics.
  • Your photo library is never read: only a photo you choose or take is used.

Your rights

As we do not keep your photos or questions, there is nothing stored about you on our side. Deleting the app deletes everything it saved on your phone. For any question about your data, including your rights under the GDPR, write to the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
