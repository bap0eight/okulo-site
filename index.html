Okulo

A colour assistant for people with red-green colour blindness.

Point your phone at something, type or say a short question ("Is it cooked?", "Which light is on?", "Do these match?") and Okulo gives a short answer, tuned to your type of colour blindness. Tap any point of the photo to hear its colour name, without internet.

Help and contact

Questions, problems or ideas: baptiste.corman08@gmail.com. We usually reply within two days.

Safety

Answers come from an AI and can be wrong, especially in poor light. Okulo describes what it sees; it never tells you to go. Never use Okulo for traffic lights while driving, and check meat with a food thermometer: colour cannot prove that it is safe.

The colour test in the app is a screen-based estimate, not a medical diagnosis. An optometrist can confirm your type of colour vision.
